The route

Interstate to the mountains on day one, then the parkway the rest of the way. Select a stop on the map or in the itinerary to see what's there.

Check parkway status the morning you drive. Sections close for ice, snow and fog with little warning, especially above 5,000 ft near Craggy Gardens and Mount Mitchell. Hurricane Helene (September 2024) also caused long repair closures in North Carolina. The NPS keeps a real-time road map.

There are no gas stations on the parkway itself. Fill up in Asheville, Blowing Rock and Fancy Gap. Many visitor centers and concessions close for the season by early November.

What to rent

Midsize SUV

The best fit for November. Higher clearance and AWD (if offered) help on steep side roads like NC-128 to Mount Mitchell and on frosty mornings.

Full-size sedan

Cheaper and better on gas for the 265-mile interstate day. The parkway is paved and well graded, so a sedan is fine in clear weather.

Convertible

Great for the overlooks from May to October. In November, mountain highs are often in the 40s and the ridges stay cold and windy.

Driving the parkway

45 mph, and you'll go slower

The speed limit is 45 or lower. Plan on 30–35 mph average once you add curves and overlook stops.

Mileposts run south to north

Concrete posts count down from MP 469 at Cherokee to MP 0 at Shenandoah. You'll drive from MP 382 down to MP 120.

Download maps offline

Cell signal drops out for long stretches between Linville and Doughton Park. Save offline maps before leaving Asheville.

Tunnels and fog

The Asheville-area section has many unlit tunnels and the ridges fog in fast. Keep sunglasses off and headlights on.
